# Customer-Behavior-Analysis
Python project using Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to analyze 250k customer transactions, find sales trends, and segment customers for business growth.

## Project Overview
In this project, I analyzed a dataset of 250,000 customer transactions using Python. The goal was to understand customer buying habits, find out why sales were dropping, and provide simple business solutions to improve revenue.

## Tools & Libraries Used
* **Python** - For writing the logic.
* **Pandas** - For data loading, data cleaning, and creating pivot tables.
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** - For creating charts (bar charts and pie charts) to visualize patterns.

## Key Findings (What I Found)
1. **Sales Drop:** Total sales for all products dropped significantly during the last 4 months of the year (September to December).
2. **Customer Segments:** The company has a very strong base of loyal customers (around 43,000 VIP and Regular customers). The new or low-value customers are very few (around 5,800).
3. **Product Returns:** Books and Clothing categories have the highest number of returns overall. However, returns actually decreased in the last 4 months because overall buying went down.

## Actionable Recommendations (My Suggestions)


* * **Keep Loyal Customers Happy (VIP & Regular):** We have 43,000+ loyal customers. The company must launch exclusive holiday rewards or gifts to keep them active and stop the Q4 sales drop.
* **Bring Back New Customers:** Target the ~5,800 low-frequency customers with specific discount codes during festive months to encourage their second purchase.
* **Product Quality Audit:** Books and Clothing have the highest annual returns (~60k combined). We must review customer feedback to fix product sizing and quality.
* **Optimize Top Payment Channels:** Credit Card and PayPal drive over 70% of total revenue (~478 Lakhs combined) but also cause the highest returns. We should offer secure checkout incentives on these modes to lower transaction failures or buyer remorse.
* **Target High-Spending Age Groups:** Middle-Aged (30-50) and Senior (>50) segments drive the maximum sales (~530 Lakhs combined), while the Youth segment spends the least (~150 Lakhs). Marketing campaigns should heavily target older groups with premium products, while launching trendy, budget-friendly campaigns to attract younger buyers.
